# HS海关编码查询 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**生效日期：2026 年 10 月 10 日**
**适用应用：HS海关编码查询（HSQuery）**，Bundle ID：`com.hsquery.app`

---

## 简体中文

### 一、概述

HS海关编码查询是一款**完全离线**的 HS 海关编码与目录树查询工具。我们不设账号系统，不要求你注册或登录，**不收集、不上传、不共享你的任何个人信息**，也不会记录你查询了什么。

### 二、在你的设备上处理的数据

以下内容全部在你的设备本地处理，不会离开设备：

- **查询内容**：你输入的商品名称或 HS 编码，仅在本机内存中用于匹配，查询结束即释放。
- **编码数据**：随安装包一起提供的 12,089 条商品编码与 16,406 个目录节点，保存在应用沙盒内的数据库文件中，只读。
- **复制操作**：当你点击复制按钮时，编码会被写入系统剪贴板，由你自行决定粘贴到何处。

我们没有自建服务器，App 内**不存在**任何向第三方上传查询记录、使用统计或崩溃日志的代码。

### 三、App 内购买

App 免费下载，通过 Apple 的 App 内购买（非消耗型项目）一次性解锁全部数据。购买与恢复购买由 **Apple** 处理，我们不接触、也拿不到你的姓名、支付方式或 Apple ID 等信息。与此相关的数据处理适用 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)。

App 会向 App Store 查询该内购项目的**价格与交易状态**，这是 App 唯一的联网行为，且只在打开解锁页面或恢复购买时发生。

### 四、系统权限

**App 不申请任何系统权限**——不使用定位、相机、相册、通讯录、日历、麦克风、通知，也不读取广告标识符（IDFA），不做任何跨 App 追踪。

### 五、儿童隐私

本应用是面向进出口、物流、报关等从业人员的工具类应用，不面向儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

### 六、数据留存与删除

由于我们不收集任何数据，也就不存在服务器端的留存。App 在本机保存的内容（内置数据库）在**卸载 App 时即被系统一并删除**。

### 七、政策更新

功能或法律要求变化时我们可能更新本政策，更新后的版本会发布在本页面并同步更新「生效日期」。

### 八、联系我们

任何隐私相关问题，请联系：**peill@me.com**

## English

### 1. Overview

HSQuery (HS海关编码查询) is a **fully offline** reference tool for HS customs codes and the tariff catalogue tree. There is no account system, no sign-up or login, and we **do not collect, upload, or share any personal information**. We do not log what you search for.

### 2. Data processed on your device

Everything below stays on your device:

- **Your queries** — the product name or HS code you type is matched in local memory only.
- **The code database** — 12,089 product codes and 16,406 catalogue nodes ship inside the app as a read-only database in the app sandbox.
- **Copy action** — tapping copy places the code on the system clipboard; where you paste it is up to you.

We operate no backend servers. The app contains **no code** that uploads query history, usage analytics, or crash logs to us or to any third party.

### 3. In-app purchase

The app is free to download and offers a single non-consumable in-app purchase that unlocks all data. Purchases and restores are handled by **Apple**; we never receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that data is governed by the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

The app asks the App Store for the **price and transaction status** of that product. This is the app's only network activity, and it happens only when you open the unlock screen or restore a purchase.

### 4. System permissions

The app **requests no system permissions** — no location, camera, photo library, contacts, calendar, microphone, or notifications. It does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and performs no cross-app tracking.

### 5. Children's privacy

This is a utility for professionals in import/export, logistics, and customs brokerage. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 6. Retention and deletion
Because we collect nothing, there is no server-side retention. Anything stored locally (the bundled database) is removed by the system when you **delete the app**.

### 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

### 8. Contact

For any privacy question, contact: **peill@me.com**
